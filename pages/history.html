THE MANIFESTO

Spanish by identity.
Competitive by ambition.

Spanish Armada is a FIFA 13 Manager Career Mode project centred on Athletic Club. The challenge is to build a competitive squad using players whose in-game nationality is Spanish, while keeping recruitment decisions grounded in a believable football context.

The archive documents more than results. It records why players arrived, why others left, how young talents developed, and how each season changed the direction of the club.

Project principles

  • Spanish nationality according to the game database.
  • Realistic transfer approach.
  • Develop youth without rushing decisions.
  • Evaluate signings with enough playing time.
  • Never buy simply to spend the budget.
  • Document facts; leave unknown statistics marked as TBD.
